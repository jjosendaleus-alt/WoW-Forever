Milestones that matter

  • Skill 20: Camp Tent. Everyone sitting nearby gets rested XP, topped up to 5% of a level. Confirmed
  • Skill 125-140: Tanning Rack.
    • 48 Leatherworking recipes can only be crafted next to a rack, including every Stalker's piece and the Trapper's legguards.
    • The first rack recipes open at 125, but you need 140 to learn and place a rack. Below 140 you can craft at another leatherworker's rack.
    • Get the blueprint from Happy Camper for 15s, just outside Ironforge at Dun Morogh 51.6, 40.0. He also sells to Horde, but Ironforge guards stand nearby. It also drops from Razorclaw the Butcher in Shadowfang Keep, very rarely.
    • Each rack costs 5 Medium Leather, 3 Fine Thread and 2 Simple Wood. It is used up when you place it and lasts 15 minutes.
    • All camp objects share a 1-hour cooldown.
    Datamined Unconfirmed (vendor and drop rate are player reports)
  • Skill 150, 225 and 300: one account-wide Legacy point each, the first time any character on the account reaches them. Confirmed
  • Skill 300: Sewing Machine (from a blueprint), plus the "Leatherworker" title from a certification costing 1,000 Merchant's Favor. Datamined

2. Hunter recipes

Hunters wear leather until level 40 and mail after. Leatherworking makes both. Many of Forever's new crafted pieces bind on pickup, so you have to make them yourself.

Merchant's Favor (new currency)

Fill "Waylaid Crate" items, which are rare monster drops and also sold on the AH, and hand them in for Merchant's Favor. A crate pays 5-20 Favor.

  • Horde: crates go to Dokimi and Leatherworking recipes come from Pawani, at Durotar Supply and Logistics just west of the Crossroads (Barrens 49.6, 29.6).
  • Alliance: Daniel Stitchsong at Three Corners, Redridge.

Hunter tip: a Journeyman Fabrics crate takes 2 Cured Medium Hide and pays 20 Favor. Datamined

Forever-specific Skinning

  • One skin per creature: since the 1 Oct beta build, a creature can be skinned once, even with several skinners in the group. Confirmed
  • Pristine Leather (new Scarce material) goes into about 126 recipes. Trapper's gear needs it.
    • One guide: skinned in Loch Modan and the Barrens, and also dropped as loot. Unconfirmed
    • A player test: only skinners at character level 25 or lower get it, from level 10-25 beasts outside starting zones. Unconfirmed
    • Either way, stock up while levelling.
  • Pristine Hide (Wetlands Maddened Rotclaws) and Pristine Scale are also new.
  • Camp Chair (skill 20; 3 Light Leather, 2 Simple Wood): 2% crit for everyone sitting nearby. It doesn't stack with Moonkin Aura. Confirmed
    • Upgrades: Field Guide (140; grants Track Beasts; blueprint from Lady Anacondra, Wailing Caverns).
    • Trapper's Workbench (300; holds a trap; blueprint from Houndmaster Loksey, Scarlet Monastery, very rare).
    Datamined
  • Keen Skinning Knife: +3 Skinning. Reward from the Horde quest "Unwelcome Guests" (Barrens, quest level 14). It counts as a skinning knife and is a caster dagger. Its item level dropped 19 to 18 in the 27 Oct build. Datamined
  • Other skill boosts: Enchant Gloves - Skinning (+5) and the new Advanced Skinning (+10); Cartilage Shapers (+4).
  • Apprentice's Skinning Satchel (4 slots) from junior trainers. Hides now stack to 20.
  • Legacy perks:
    • Bountiful Harvest: +20% Scarce materials per rank, 5 ranks.
    • Working Overtime: +4% skill-up chance per rank, 5 ranks.
    • Performance Bonus: 5/10/15% chance of double crate Favor.
    • Dedicated Study: +1 to your lowest tradeskill, 23h cooldown.
    • Skinning itself earns no Legacy points.
    Confirmed (perks exist) Datamined (values)

6. When to level, and pairing

  • Pairing: Leatherworking + Skinning while levelling. Every major guide agrees, and Hunters kill beasts all day anyway. Common endgame swap: Engineering + Mining.
  • Level 1-10: take Skinning at once (book or junior trainer) and skin everything. Learn Leatherworking by level 5-10 and push it to 50.
  • Level 10: train Journeyman in both. Start filling crates for Favor.
  • Levels 10-20: aim for LW 125 by level 20, when Expert opens, and 140 for the Tanning Rack. Buy Trapper's patterns as your Favor allows: hood first (wear level 20), then boots, gloves, belt. Hoard Pristine Leather while you are level 25 or lower.
  • Levels 30-40: Heavy Quiver or Ammo Pouch and the Stalker's belt at 30. LW 200 for Stalker's Mail Boots and the Quickdraw Quiver at 40.
  • Level 35: Artisan training.

7. Recent changes (up to 31 Oct)

  • 27 Oct notes: the official notes from 26-27 Oct only cover racial abilities, with no profession changes. Orc Command (+5% Hunter pet damage) is back, and Troll Berserking now lasts 15s. Confirmed
  • Launch client build 1.60.1.70707 (30 Oct):
    • Aspect of the Hawk cut roughly in half (rank 5: 90 to 47 attack power).
    • Trueshot Aura rank 3: 50 to 30.
    • No Leatherworking or Skinning recipe changes.
    Datamined
  • 30 Oct server hotfixes (unannounced):
    • The servers now send 36 recipe entries, including tradeable patterns for the Trapper's, Brawler's and other sets: armor at 75, pants at 100, helm at 125.
    • Toughened Leather Armor becomes an uncommon bind-on-equip item with Stamina and Spirit.
    Unconfirmed
  • 16 Oct: vendor sell prices reduced on many profession-crafted items (amount not given). Legacy Bartering no longer discounts training or repairs. Confirmed
  • 1 Oct:
    • One skin per creature restored.
    • Dungeon-drop Blacksmithing and Leatherworking recipes made available.
    • Fewer Poor Copper Veins in starter areas.
    Confirmed
  • Auction houses: Forever has no realms. Players are grouped into ruleset pools (Normal, PvP, RP), each with a fresh economy at launch. Confirmed Blizzard has not announced a Game Data API namespace for Forever auctions. Fan price sites rely on player-uploaded scans.
