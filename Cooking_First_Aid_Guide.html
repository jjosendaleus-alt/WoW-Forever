1. Cooking ranks and trainers

Cooking is a secondary profession, so it doesn't use one of your two primary slots. In each starting area, the level 4 quest "Camping 101: Cooking" sends you to the local cook. You start knowing Basic Campfire, Charred Wolf Meat, Herb Baked Egg and Roasted Boar Meat.

2. Campfires and camp cooking

  • You need a campfire to cook. Cooking fires sit next to most trainers, and in Forever the fire is an item you craft and place. Confirmed
  • Basic Campfire Kit (skill 1): 1 Simple Wood (38c). Crafting it needs a Flint and Tinder in your bags (1s 35c); placing it doesn't. It holds 3 more camp objects and has a 5-minute cooldown.
    • Horde wood and tinder: Xen'to (Orgrimmar), Ronald Burch (Undercity), Naal Mistrunner (Thunder Bluff), Jark (Razor Hill barracks), Moorat Longstride (Bloodhoof), Mrs. Winters (Brill), Aza'bek (Barrens).
    • Alliance: Erika Tate (Stormwind), Emrul Riknussun (Ironforge), Fyldan (Darnassus), Brog Hamfist (Goldshire), Kreg Bilmn (Kharanos), Nyoma (Dolanaar).
  • Journeyman Campfire (blueprint; Star Wood; 5 objects), Expert Campfire (blueprint; Thick Logs; 10 objects), Cookie's Feast (140, blueprint; Stamina food for the camp) and Iron Oven (300, blueprint; needed for 18 recipes). Sources disagree on the campfire skill levels: Journeyman 90 or 140, Expert 200 or 220. Unconfirmed
  • To get a camp object's buff, sit or craft nearby for 1 minute. The buff lasts 1 hour, and all camp objects share a 1-hour cooldown. Confirmed
  • Since 24 Sep you can also earn the buff while lying down, sleeping or kneeling, and you can craft while you wait. Confirmed

9. First Aid in Forever: it exists, and it grew

First Aid is still in WoW Forever. It is one of three secondary professions, with Cooking and Fishing, and doesn't take a primary slot. It was expanded, not removed. It was shown in Blizzard's show-floor demo, and the beta client lists 31 recipes. Datamined Blizzard has not put out First Aid patch notes.

  • Healing Potions (Minor to Major) moved from Alchemy to First Aid, and now also take spices. Alchemy keeps only "Discolored" healing potions, which heal but hurt you afterwards. Datamined
  • New curatives:
    • Poultices cure diseases.
    • Tourniquets cure bleeds, which almost nobody else can remove.
    • A fuller Anti-Venom line cures poisons.
  • Three camp objects: First Aid Kit (20), Toxin Study (140) and Plague Doctor's Laboratory (300).
  • 31 recipes in the client, 17 of them new. First Aid earns no Legacy points.

14. Legacy perks and Merchant's Favor

Legacy perks (account-wide)

  • Master Chef: 10-50% chance of an extra Cooking result, 5 ranks.
  • Gourmand: food buffs last 33/67/100% longer.
  • Field Medicine: shortens Recently Bandaged by 5/10 sec, outside dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.
  • Luremaster: extra fish chance while a lure is on.
  • Working Overtime: +4% skill-up chance per rank, any tradeskill.
  • Dedicated Study: +1 to your lowest primary or secondary tradeskill, 23h cooldown. It works well on Cooking and First Aid.
  • Permanence: camp rest benefits last 50% longer.
  • Cooking, First Aid and Fishing earn no Legacy points.

Confirmed (perks exist) Datamined (values)

Merchant's Favor (Cooking)

  • Horde: Aza'bek at Durotar Supply and Logistics, just west of the Crossroads (Barrens 49.6, 29.2).
  • Alliance: Kalsey Sanden at Three Corners, Redridge (10.8, 72.4).
  • They sell six smoothie recipes: Venomous (25), Slimy (100) and Mrrggl Smrrthle (125) for 30 Favor each; Calcified (180) and Spicy (200) for 60; Wicked (250) for 90.
  • Favor comes from filling "Waylaid Crate" items and handing them in sealed.
  • No First Aid Favor recipes are listed.
