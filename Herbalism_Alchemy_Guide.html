WoW Forever: Herbalism & Alchemy

Horde-first guide for the 4 Nov 2026 launch, with Hunter notes

Last updated 31 Oct 2026, pre-launch, based on beta client data. The beta stopped at character level 30 and skill 225, so anything above 225 has not been tested in play. Everything here can still change at launch.

Badge legend

  • Confirmed Stated in Blizzard's own posts or patch notes.
  • Datamined Read from the beta or launch client files by fan sites, not yet announced or verified in play.
  • Unconfirmed Player reports, one source only, or sources that disagree.
  • Classic-inferred Taken from Classic data; not shown in Forever data yet.

Unless marked otherwise, recipe, herb-skill, reagent and skill-color numbers are Datamined from beta builds 1.60.1.70124 to 70707. Colors are written as orange / yellow / green / grey: the skill at which a recipe turns each color.

4. Forever-only Herbalism

  • Scarce materials: Frilled Lichen (from Earthroot, Mageroyal, Briarthorn and Stranglekelp, plus Peacebloom per testers), Rosecap (Kingsblood, Fadeleaf, Goldthorn) and Stranglevine (higher-level herbs, not pinned down). They drop while you pick. They go into Alchemy dyes (Cerulean, Viridian and a rosecap-based dye) and some enchants. Unconfirmed (drop sources)
  • Herb Seeds in four tiers (Commonplace, Uncommon, Unusual, Scarce) drop from herbs.
  • Camp objects are made in a new "Gardening" window; each craft makes 2.
    • Incense Candle (20; 1 Peacebloom + 1 Silverleaf): Intellect for everyone sitting nearby, 25 at level 60. It doesn't stack with Arcane Intellect.
    • Greenhouse (140; blueprint from Shadetooth, Excavation Site: Wetlands): plant 1 seed and pick the herb about 8 minutes later. The Greenhouse lasts about 15 minutes, so you get one planting. Seen so far: Commonplace seeds grow Peacebloom or Earthroot, Uncommon grow Silverleaf or Kingsblood, Scarce grow Ghost Mushroom. Unconfirmed (tester reports)
    • Seed Hybridizer (300): combine 2 seeds of a tier to multiply them or upgrade the tier.
    • All camp objects share a 1-hour cooldown.
    Confirmed (system) Datamined (values)
  • Bags: new Apprentice's Herb Pouch (4 slots) from many trade vendors. Cenarion Herb Bag is 26 slots (20 in Classic) and Satchel of Cenarius is 32 (24 in Classic).
  • No Legacy points for gathering skill. Only the six crafting professions earn them.

9. Healing potions moved to First Aid

  • In Forever, Minor through Major Healing Potion are First Aid recipes, with spices added to the reagents. Every character can make them. Several fan sites agree, and the beta client lists them under First Aid. Datamined One database still lists the old Alchemy healing-potion entries. Another says those spells are gone from the client and only skill-table stubs remain. Unconfirmed
  • Alchemy instead makes Discolored Healing Potions in six ranks: Minor (quest recipe), Lesser 85, regular 135, Greater 175, Superior 230 and Major. Each needs a Murloc Eye plus a herb.
    • Example: Discolored Healing Potion (Bruiseweed, Murloc Eye, Leaded Vial) heals 600-680, then you take 75% of that back over time as a 6-sec poison or a 12-sec disease.
    • Since 8 Oct, that damage no longer breaks crowd control. Confirmed
  • For Hunters, this means regular healing potions come from your own First Aid, not from Alchemy.

10. Transmutes and specializations

  • Specializations: none in Forever. Potion, Elixir and Transmutation Master came with The Burning Crusade and are not included. Two fan sites say so; Blizzard hasn't. Unconfirmed
  • Transmutes exist Datamined:
    • Iron to Gold and Mithril to Truesilver (225).
    • Arcanite (275).
    • Elemental essences: Air to Fire, Fire to Earth, Earth to Water, Water to Air, Earth to Life, Life to Earth, Undeath to Water, Water to Undeath (275).
    • Elemental Fire (300).
    • New: Transmute: Legionite (275; Heavy Thorium Bar + Fel Crystal).
  • Stones: Philosopher's Stone (225). Alchemist's Stone can be learned at 275 (300 in Classic). It replaces a Philosopher's Stone for transmutes and boosts healing and mana potions. Its source isn't known.
  • Cooldowns: the launch client changed a value on several transmute spells; no source explains it. Datamined Expect Classic-style cooldowns until tested. Classic-inferred

11. Legacy perks and Merchant's Favor

Legacy perks (account-wide)

  • Working Overtime: +4% skill-up chance per rank, 5 ranks, any tradeskill.
  • Dedicated Study: +1 to your lowest primary or secondary tradeskill, 23h cooldown. At all-300 it gives 2-4 elemental essences instead.
  • Bountiful Harvest: +20% Scarce materials per rank, 5 ranks. It doesn't add extra herbs.
  • Performance Bonus: 5/10/15% chance of double crate Favor.
  • Reagent Economy: the Incense Candle costs no reagents.

Confirmed (perks exist) Datamined (values)

Merchant's Favor

Fill "Waylaid Crate" items (world drops from level 10, also on the AH) and hand them in sealed for Favor. Hand-ins and Alchemy recipes are at:

  • Horde: Durotar Supply and Logistics just west of the Crossroads. Apothecary Durelle sells the Alchemy recipes (Barrens 49.8, 29.4).
  • Alliance: Azeroth Commerce Authority, Three Corners, Redridge. Nina Surefire sells the Alchemy recipes (10.8, 72.4).
  • Herb crates take one bundle per crate, e.g. 20 Peacebloom, 20 Silverleaf, 10 Kingsblood, 20 Liferoot or 20 Dreamfoil. Bands: Apprentice and Journeyman (level 10), Expert (20), Artisan (35).
  • Alchemy Favor recipes (28 plus the Certification), including:
    • 45 Favor, skill 65-140: Lesser Mageblood, Lesser Intellect, Lesser Cleric's, Mender's, Spellblasting, Frenzy.
    • 120 Favor, 165-245: Greater Spirit, Greater Mageblood, Greater Fortitude, Dragonfire.
    • 180 Favor, 250-285: Phalanx, Cunning, Ferocity, Sages, Whale, Owl, Nature Power, Wicked Regeneration.
    • 240 Favor, 290-300: Major Frenzy, Grizzly.

Datamined

12. Recent changes (up to 31 Oct)

  • Launch client build 1.60.1.70707 (30 Oct):

  • 24 Sep: Cultivation now needs player level of at least the herb's skill requirement divided by 5. Confirmed
